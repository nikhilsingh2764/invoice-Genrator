A production-ready **MERN Stack Invoice Management System** designed for businesses to create, manage, generate, and share professional invoices.

The platform provides complete invoice lifecycle management with:

- Secure authentication
- Role-based protected APIs
- PDF invoice generation
- Email delivery automation
- Redis caching
- Rate limiting
- Data validation
- Scalable backend architecture

# 📌 Project Overview

InvoicePilot helps small businesses manage their complete billing workflow digitally.

Users can:

- Create business profiles
- Manage customers
- Manage products/services
- Generate invoices
- Download invoice PDFs
- Send invoices through email
- Track payment status
- Search, filter and paginate invoice records


---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional invoice management has several problems:

- Manual invoice creation
- Spreadsheet dependency
- Difficult customer tracking
- No automated PDF generation
- No email-based invoice delivery


## Solution

InvoicePilot provides a centralized invoice management platform where businesses can handle their billing operations from one dashboard.
